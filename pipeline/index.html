CriptoKnyght Pipeline

A personal video-editing tool for the CriptoKnyght YouTube channel.

What it is. CriptoKnyght Pipeline is a private tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It turns the owner's recorded gameplay into edited, narrated episodes and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel as private videos for review. It isn't a public service, it has no other users, and nobody else can sign in to it.

How it uses YouTube

  • Lists the channel's own uploads, to find new raw recordings the owner has marked private.
  • Uploads finished episodes to the same channel, as private videos, with their title, description, thumbnail and captions.
  • Changes a video's visibility only when the owner tells it to publish one.

It only ever acts on the owner's own channel, and only after the owner signs in with Google and approves access.
